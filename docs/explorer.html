    How to read this page
    • What a PMF is here: −kT ln P(r) at 300 K of the raw histogram of a centre-of-mass distance (cpptraj, Å converted to nm), with no r² volume (Jacobian) correction, exactly as pmf_from_cpptraj.py computes it. Each curve is shifted to 0 at the populated bin nearest 1.8 nm and cut there, so a negative value means “more probable than at 1.8 nm”.
    • Residue pairs: PMF of the centre-of-mass distance between position i of peptide 1 (rows) and position j of peptide 2 (columns); 36 ordered pairs, 0.01 nm bins. X = residue 6 (E, G, H, K, Q or R depending on the system). In the symmetric systems (i, j) and (j, i) are equivalent, so their difference is a second convergence check. LIVAGE (E⁻/E⁰) is not symmetric: residue 6 is GLU (−1) on peptide 1 and GLH (neutral) on peptide 2.
    • 3-residue windows: LIV, IVA, VAG, AGX; off-diagonal cells pool both orderings (window a of peptide 1 with b of peptide 2 and vice versa); 0.02 nm bins. LIVA fragment: residues 2–5 vs 10–13.
    • Mean ± band: mean of the first and second trajectory halves (each with the first 5% of all frames dropped) on a common grid; the band is |PMF1 − PMF2|/2, a quick convergence check (click the band entry under the chart to hide or show it). Full: whole-trajectory PMF after dropping the first 5% of frames (pairs only). Halves: both halves drawn, solid = half 1, dashed = half 2.
    • PMF axis range: Fit zooms on the wells of the shown curves; Shared uses one range for every PMF of the family so you can compare depths between cells; Full also shows the repulsive wall.
    • Keyboard: every control is a button; arrow keys move inside the matrix; focus the chart and use ←/→ (Shift = 5 bins) to move the read-out. Double-click a system chip to show only that system. The URL remembers the view, so you can bookmark or share it.
    • Data: overall/data/<system>/<SEQ>_PMF/ CSV snapshots written by pmf_from_cpptraj.py, exported to docs/assets/pmf_data.js by overall/scripts/export_explorer_data.py.
